The opportunity

A startup concept for affordable, approachable cybersecurity aimed at UAE SMEs, freelancers, remote workers, and individuals. The proposal responds to limited security expertise, budget constraints, password reuse, and concern about data breaches.

Proposed product

The proposed mobile and desktop experience includes phishing detection, password management, two-step verification, secure communication, customized alerts, and an “Ask Amen” AI assistant. Arabic support and simple security guidance are part of the intended experience.

Business development

The coursework includes personas, empathy mapping, brainstorming, competitive analysis, survey and interview material, prototype concepts, feasibility discussion, and a freemium/subscription business model.

Revenue, user-growth, and break-even figures in the deck are forecasts. Proposed capabilities and market-positioning claims are not presented as a launched or independently validated product.
